    General
    Intro

    Promo intro: the theme color colors the logo and the light. Small cube, tiles, trail, drop, camera and the SMB easter egg do nothing. It plays promo.wav if present.

    Jingle volume
    Delay

    Delay: black screen before the animation, for slow HDMI screens and mods. It also gives a Wii Remote time to connect for the SMB easter egg.

    SMB easter egg

    Super Mario Bros.: needs your own smb.nes next to boot.dol. Press B early in the animation to play.

    Logo & glass cube
    Glass flashes

    Without a logo color, the logo continues the tile pattern (the next tile in cycle mode, the last one in spread mode), or uses the theme color. The glass cube follows the logo unless it has its own color.

    Background & text

    Without a text color, NINTENDO and the logotype are white on dark backgrounds and black on light ones.

    Tiles, in the order the cube lays them

    Colors are computed with the program's own code (recolor.c and the color part of scene.c), so they match what the Wii applies. Cube, logo, drop and glass show what each target does to the models' indigo (95,80,190).

    Color timeline

    Columns are stages, not frames: the middle of each roll, then the final jump. A hatched cell is a tile not laid yet.

    Pixel view

    The Wii keeps only brightness × opacity of the image. Bright pixels show the text color, darker ones let the background through, so a dark shadow or outline disappears.

    Export

    Saving isn't available in this view. Right-click or long-press the image to save it.

    352 × 40, gray on black: the size of the texture it replaces, so the program uses it pixel for pixel. It is also part of the zip on the Animation tab.
